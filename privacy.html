The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. The font is hosted on this site, so loading it sends nothing to third parties.

Emailing me

If you email info@toucancreative.tech, Cloudflare Email Routing forwards the message to my Gmail account (Google). I use your email and what you write only to reply and to deal with your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, steps before a possible contract, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, answering enquiries).

The free design tool

The design tool turns your answers to 8 questions into a sculpture concept, and on request emails you mock-up images and a PDF of the design. Using it is voluntary. Here is what happens to your data at each step.

Your answers

Your answers are kept in your browser's session storage while you use the tool, so you can go back and change them. They're deleted when you close the tab. No cookies are used.

When you finish, your answers are sent to the tool's server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). The server sends them to Anthropic (Anthropic PBC, USA), whose AI model Claude writes and checks the concept. Please don't put personal details in your answers: describe your product, not people.

The bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile)

To stop automated abuse, the tool uses Cloudflare Turnstile, an invisible check that you're a person. Turnstile processes technical data from your browser and device, including your IP address. This protects the tool from bots and from running up costs (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum describes what it collects and how it's used.

Daily limits

To enforce the daily limits, the server stores a one-way scrambled code (a hash) made from your IP address and the date, never your IP address itself. These counters are deleted automatically after 2 days (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, preventing abuse).

Your concept

The finished concept and your answers are kept on the server for 7 days, so mock-up images can be made from them. They're also added to a Google Sheet (Google) and sent to me by email, so I can see how the tool is used and follow up on interesting concepts (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Mock-up images and the design PDF

If you ask for mock-up images, you give your email address. To check it's yours, I first email you a link to click. Only after you click it:

  • the concept text (not your email address) is sent to Google's Gemini API (Google), which makes 2 images;
  • the images and a PDF of the design are emailed to you through Resend, an email delivery service (USA);
  • your email address is added to the concept's row in the Google Sheet, and I get a copy by email.

The link works for 24 hours. The images and PDF stay online at unguessable addresses for 30 days, then they're deleted. Your copy in the email doesn't expire.

I use your email address to send what you asked for and to reply to you about your concept (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, at your request and as a step before a possible contract). I don't add you to a newsletter, and I never share or sell your details.

How long the Google Sheet keeps data

Rows in the Google Sheet (answers, concept, and your email if you gave it) are kept for up to 2 years after our last contact, then deleted, unless we start working together, when the usual business record-keeping rules apply. You can ask me to delete yours at any time.

Videos

YouTube videos only load from YouTube (in its privacy-enhanced mode) when you click play, and Vimeo videos load with Vimeo's "do not track" setting. When a video loads, the provider processes your IP address and device data.

Your rights

  • get a copy of the data I hold about you (Art. 15)
  • have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17)
