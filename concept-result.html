Is it really free?

Yes. You get your concept, technical outline and images without paying anything or booking a call.

Could the sculpture actually be built?

The technical outline only uses parts I work with, so it's a realistic starting point. The images are concept sketches, not a final design.

Do I need to give my email?

Not for the concept: it appears on the page straight away. Your email is only needed if you'd like mock-up images of your sculpture and a PDF of the whole design. I'll send you a link to check it's your email, and once you click it they're emailed to you.

What happens to my answers?

They're used only to make your concept and to follow up about it. They're never shared or sold. Details are in the privacy policy.

What if I want to take it further?

I can turn the concept into a finished design for a few hundred euros. If you're not happy with it, for any reason, you get your money back.
