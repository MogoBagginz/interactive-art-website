Questions.

Something else? Just ask — email me.

How long does a prototype take?

Prototypes are kept deliberately small and quick. We agree the scope and timescale up front.

What do I get at the end?

A working prototype, plus a short summary of what we learned and what I’d recommend next.

Can the prototype become the final piece?

Sometimes — but usually it’s a learning tool. The final build is then made robust for real-world use.

Do I have to do the full build with you?

No. The prototype is useful on its own, though I’d be glad to carry the project through.

Not sure it’s possible?

Describe the idea and the part you’re unsure about. That’s exactly where a prototype starts.
