Questions.

Something else? Just ask — email me.

Do we need to bring an artist?

Often an artist is already involved. If not, get in touch anyway — we can talk about how to shape the idea.

Will it run without anyone looking after it?

That’s the aim. Pieces are designed to start up on their own and run day after day with minimal attention.

What happens if something goes wrong later?

You get documentation of how the system works, and we can agree ongoing support if you need it.

How long does a commission take?

It depends on scale. Tell me your deadline early and I’ll be honest about what’s achievable.

Planning a commission?

Tell me about the space, the occasion and your timeline. I’ll come back with questions and next steps.
