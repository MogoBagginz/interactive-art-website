Interactive Sculpture

A collaboration between artist, engineer and technology.

01 / The idea

How do you turn an artistic intention into a reliable physical interaction?

The sculpture was conceived as a physical artwork that responds to its environment. The technical challenge was not simply to make electronics function, but to make the interaction feel natural and integral to the work rather than like a system attached to it.

02 / Collaboration

You remain the artist.

My role is not to define what the artwork should be. It is to understand what the artist is trying to achieve and explore what technology can do to support that vision.

Meet Murali.

I’m Murali Wood, an embedded systems engineer and the founder of Toucan Creative Technology.

I started Toucan because I believe technology can make physical art more engaging, surprising and alive. I’m interested in the point where engineering meets creativity: where electronics stop being components and become movement, light, sound, interaction and experience.

Toucan exists to create interesting art — and to help artists and creative teams realise ideas that would otherwise be difficult to make.
