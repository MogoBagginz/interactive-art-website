01 ART × TECHNOLOGY

Your idea.
The technology to make it possible.

Maybe it should move, respond, light up, react to people, make sound, or behave in a way that isn't immediately obvious.

That's where I come in. I work alongside artists and creative teams to design and build the technology that makes interactive work possible — while keeping the creative vision at the centre.

T

Technical expertise
without taking over
the creative process.

I collaborate with artists to understand what they're trying to achieve, explore what's technically possible, and find practical ways of making it happen.

The goal isn't to turn an artistic idea into an engineering project. It's to use engineering to make the artistic idea possible.

MW

I'm Murali.

I'm an embedded systems engineer interested in the space between technology, objects and art.

I started Toucan Creative Technology to work with people who have ambitious creative ideas and need someone to make the technical side possible.

I believe technology shouldn't make interactive art feel more complicated. It should make more interesting art possible.
