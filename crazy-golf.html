Ideas for your course.

Hole-in-one celebrations

Moving obstacles

Motorised features that spin, swing or open — timed, random or triggered by the ball.

Themed effects

Glowing lava, rumbling volcanoes, flashing lighthouses — effects that fit your theme.

Ball detection

Sensors that know when a ball passes a point, enters a tunnel or lands in a hole.

Indoor & night lighting

Lighting that changes the atmosphere of a course, or responds to play.

Built for the public

Robust, splash-resistant where needed, and easy for staff to switch on and reset.

Questions.

Something else? Just ask — email me.

Can you add effects to an existing course?

Yes. Effects can often be fitted to existing holes, as well as designed into new courses.

Does it work outdoors?

Outdoor features need weather-protected electronics, and we design for that from the start.

What if something gets damaged?

Parts are chosen to be tough and accessible, so problems can be found and fixed quickly.

Do you build the themed scenery too?

I focus on the electronics and software, and work alongside your themers and set builders.
